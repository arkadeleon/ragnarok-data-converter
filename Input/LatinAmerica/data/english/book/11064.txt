---- **Chapter of Ardur, the Fire Spirit** ----

The summoned high fire spirit, Ardur, grants the summoner a special fire power,  
and takes different individual actions through the summoner's "Spirit Control" ability.

**Passive Effect:** Fire property magic damage +10%, increases the MATK of [Conflagration].

^fa8072**[Spirit Control Level 1: Passive Mode]**^000000  
"Summoner, I bestow upon you the power of flame."

**[Flame Technique]**  
i. Increases the MATK of the summoner's Fire Bolt by 400%.  
ii. When the summoner performs a normal physical attack, it triggers Level 1 Fire Bolt with a 7% chance.  
   (If a higher level has been learned, it triggers at that level.)  
iii. The attack property of Psychic Wave becomes Fire property.

^0000ff**[Spirit Control Level 2: Defensive Mode]**^000000  
"Summoner, receive the blessing of fire."

**[Flame Armor]**  
i. Grants Fire property to the summoner's armor.  
ii. Summoner's Fire resistance +100, Water resistance -30.

^ff0000**[Spirit Control Level 3: Offensive Mode]**^000000  
"I will handle it myself."

**[Flame Rock]**  
i. Creates an instantaneous great flame in a 7x7 area around the target and Ardur, dealing MATK 2400% Fire property magic damage to enemies within the area, ignoring the target's magic defense.  
   (Damage is additionally increased based on the summoner's base level.)
