What is a ^FF0000Potion^000000?

A single-use dose of medicine or poison.
A potion generally refers to a single-use recovery item.
This book explains how to make red, yellow, white, and blue potions,
as well as Anti Painment and Aloe Vera.
The appendix on Embryo creation is an essential element
for alchemists who have studied bioethics.


^0000FFRed Potion^000000: Restores approximately 45 HP.
Materials: ^4B00821 Red Herb, 1 Empty Potion Bottle^000000
Manufacturing Method: Prepare a mortar.
          Place the herb into the mortar and grind it.
          Gradually add clean water while continuing to grind the herb.
          Pour into an empty potion bottle.
          For a simpler method,
          prepare this book and the materials and use the Pharmacy skill.

^008040Aloe Vera^000000: Temporarily lowers defense and increases attack power.
Materials: ^4B00821 Aloe, 1 Empty Bottle, 1 Honey^000000
Manufacturing Method: Prepare a mortar.
          Place the honey and aloe into the mortar and grind them.
          Gradually add a small amount of clean water while stirring,
          then pour into an empty bottle.
          For a simpler method, prepare this book and the materials and use the Pharmacy skill.

^FF0000Embryo^000000: Creates life in the form of an embryo of something.
Materials: ^4B00821 Yggdrasil Dew, 1 Seed of Life, 1 Glass Tube^000000
Manufacturing Method: The foundation of alchemy is the mortar.
          Prepare a mortar.
          Place the Yggdrasil Dew and Seed of Life into the mortar,
          then use the Pharmacy skill to store it in a glass tube.
          Further use requires study of bioethics.
          This is extremely dangerous and requires sufficient research.

^0000FFHomunculus Nutrition^000000: A nutritional supplement to increase intimacy with a homunculus.

Manufacturing Method: The foundation of alchemy is the mortar.

          then use the Pharmacy skill to place them into an empty bottle and shake to mix.
