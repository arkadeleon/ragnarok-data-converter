^FF0000Special Bear Paw Dish:^000000 
Materials: ^4B00821 Bear Paw, 1 Carrot, 1 Pumpkin Head, 1 Aloe Leaf,
         1 Hinare Leaf, 1 Light Sauce^000000
Cooking Method: Wash the bear paw that has been soaked in water clean,
          blanch it briefly in boiling water, then wash it clean once more,
          and carve "+"-shaped cuts into the fleshy part, then wrap it in cloth.
          Place it in a pot and boil with water, salt, refined rice wine, and pepper,
          remove the foam, then add ginger and scallions and simmer over low heat until tender.
          Cut the carrot and pumpkin into bite-sized pieces and boil to prepare.
          Wash the leaves clean, stir-fry them with a little salt,
          place half in the center of the plate,
          remove the steamed bear paw from the cloth and place it on top.
          Arrange the other half around the plate and garnish with the boiled carrot and pumpkin.
          Season the remaining bear-paw broth with light sauce, boil until it thickens,
          finish with aromatic oil, and pour it over the bear paw.
          Because bear paw can have a strong odor, take care during cooking:
          remove impurities and hair thoroughly, but trim carefully so the outer surface is not damaged,
          and change the water often to remove the smell.
          In particular, controlling the heat while boiling bear paw is important.

^0000FFRoyal Exclusive Premium Tea:^000000 A different flavor depending on the blending method!
Materials: ^4B00821 Thorny Fruit, 1 Hinare Leaf, 1 Yggdrasil Leaf, 1 Aloe Leaf,
         1 Royal Jelly, 1 Yellow Spice^000000
Cooking Method: Roast the well-dried thorny fruit and leaves, cut them into appropriate sizes,
          then blend appropriately using leaves that have completed fermentation and drying.
          If you want a tangy taste, use a bit more thorny fruit,
          and if you want a bitter taste, add more Hinare leaves.
          Pour water just before boiling into a teapot,
          steep small tea leaves for 2–3 minutes and large tea leaves for about 3–4 minutes.
          Adjust royal jelly and yellow spice to taste.

^800000Special Toast:^000000 Different from street toast (in ingredients)!
Materials: ^4B00821 Bread, 1 Royal Jelly, 1 Meat, 1 Strawberry, 1 Spicy Sauce, 1 Cheese, 1 Sweet Sauce^000000
Cooking Method: Toast the bread in butter until golden.
          Finely mince the meat and mix it with spicy sauce,
          then form thick patties sized to match the bread and pan-fry them.
          Make strawberry jam, or prepare the strawberries thinly sliced.
          Spread jam on the bread, place the meat patty on top, drizzle spicy sauce,
          then add cheese.
          If using thin slices, spread spicy sauce on the bread, add the patty,
          mix sweet sauce with royal jelly and spread it on top, then add cheese,
          place thinly sliced strawberries on top, and cover with bread.

^008040Super Spicy Curry +9:^000000 So spicy it stings your tongue when you lick it!
Materials: ^4B00821 Aloe Vera, 1 Spicy Sauce, 1 Meat, 1 Hinare Leaf,
         1 Pumpkin Head, 1 Carrot^000000
Cooking Method: Dry the aloe vera and hinare and grind into powder.
          Cut the meat into 2 cm cubes and season lightly with salt, pepper, and so on.
          Dice the pumpkin and carrot as well, melt butter in a pan, and stir-fry.
          Stir-fry the meat separately from the vegetables; when cooked, add it to the cooked vegetables
          and stir-fry a little more, then add appropriately blended aloe vera and hinare powder,
          stir-fry, add water, boil and reduce appropriately,
          then add spicy sauce and bring to a brief boil once more.

^FF8040Giant Wrap Dish:^000000 So big it’s hard to eat alone!
Materials: ^4B00821 Leaf Garment, 1 Meat, 1 Rainbow Carrot, 1 Pumpkin Head, 1 Light Sauce, 1 Grain^000000
Cooking Method: Remove fibrous strands from the stem side of the leaf garment,
          steam it with the pumpkin in a steamer, then cool in cold water.
          Drain blood from the meat, tie it with string,
          and boil it in water with pumpkin and carrot for about 40 minutes.
          You may add a little light sauce as well.
          When it is fully cooked through, remove, let it cool slightly, then slice thinly into appropriate sizes.
          Hull the grain and cook rice; julienne the carrot and lightly stir-fry it.
          Spread out the leaf, and fold the stem inward on the inside of the leaf.
          Add rice, meat, carrot, pumpkin, and so on, spread light sauce, then fold and wrap.

^400080Strawberry-Flavored Rice Ball:^000000 Some even call it a Poring-flavored rice ball.
Materials: ^4B00821 Strawberry, 1 Grain, 1 Leaf Garment, 1 Yellow Spice, 1 Red Spice, 1 Light Sauce^000000
Cooking Method: Hull the grain and cook rice; extract strawberry juice.
          Put the fluffy cooked rice into a wide bowl and let it cool appropriately,
          then add light sauce, spices, and strawberry juice and mix evenly.
          Be careful not to mix too strongly, or the rice grains will be crushed.
          Shape into bite-sized portions,
          then wrap with lightly blanched leaf garment.
