^0000FFMastela Fruit Liquor:^000000 No drunken Peco herding! A lovely purple drink
Materials: ^4B00821 Mastela Fruit, 1 Blue Potion, 1 Alcohol, 1 Lemon, 1 Yellow Spice^000000 
Cooking Method: Use a squeezer (a tool for extracting fruit juice) to juice the Mastela fruit and the lemon separately.
          Put 1 serving of alcohol and lemon juice into a blender and mix for 20 seconds,
          then add the blue potion and 1 more serving of alcohol,
          add Mastela juice, and mix for 5 seconds.
          Pour the mixed Mastela liquor into a glass, sprinkle yellow spice on top,
          and serve with half a sliced Mastela fruit.

^800000Green Salad:^000000 A table full of greenery! Full of sweet dressing~
Materials: ^4B00821 Hinare Leaf, 1 Aloe Leaf, 1 Sharp Leaf, 1 Large Leaf,
        1 Sweet Sauce, 1 Yellow Spice^000000 
Cooking Method: Pluck Hinare leaves one by one, shred them, soak in cold water, then drain.
          Trim the sharp leaf to only the tender inner stem, cut into 5 cm lengths,
          shred, soak in cold water, then drain.
          Choose a large leaf (radish) of a large size, slice it into rounds,
          shred, soak in cold water, then drain.
          Remove the thorns from the aloe leaf, split it, shred it, rinse in water, and drain.
          Remove all moisture from all prepared vegetables.
          Arrange the vegetables neatly side by side on a plate,
          top with sweet sauce before serving, and lightly sprinkle yellow spice.

^008040Bat Wing Pumpkin Steam:^000000 The bat wings won’t sprout and fly away from the pumpkin steam.
Materials: ^4B00821 Red Bat Wing, 1 Pumpkin Head, 1 Pot, 1 Hinare Leaf, 1 Red Herb^000000 
Cooking Method: Cut the bat wing into bite-sized pieces, trim and roughly chop the Hinare leaf,
          and finely chop the red herb.
          Add olive oil to a pan and stir-fry the bat wing, then add Hinare leaf and stir-fry a bit more.
          When the bat wing is cooked appropriately, add shredded red herb and season with salt.
          Split the pumpkin lengthwise, cut into 6 pieces, scrape out seeds and flesh,
          top with the stir-fried wing, and steam in a steamer that has come up to steam for about 15 minutes.

^FF8040Spicy Baked Dumplings:^000000 Crispy transparent dumpling skin!
Materials: ^4B00821 Dumpling, 1 Yellow Herb, 1 Spicy Sauce, 1 Red Spice, 1 Green Herb^000000 
Cooking Method: Cut the yellow and green herbs into pieces slightly larger than the dumpling.
          Deep-fry the dumpling and drain the oil.
          Lay green herb on a plate, place yellow herb and the dumpling on top, then drizzle spicy sauce.
          Finish and serve with red spice.

^400080Stir-Fried Scorpion:^000000 Do not judge the taste by appearance alone!
Materials: ^4B00821 Scorpion Tail, 1 Short Leg, 1 Large Leaf, 1 Old Frying Pan, 1 Cooking Oil^000000 
Cooking Method: Julienne the large leaf and set aside.
          Heat a pan over medium heat, add 2 tablespoons of cooking oil,
          then add the short leg and stir-fry.
          In another pan, stir-fry the scorpion tail in the same way.
          (Stir-fry separately so the flavors do not mix.)
          When lightly golden, add the shredded leaf and stir-fry just until it wilts slightly.
          Line a plate with the large leaf, place the stir-fried scorpion tail and short leg on top,
          and serve with soy sauce or similar.
