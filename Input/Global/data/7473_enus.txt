^FF0000Seasoned Flipper Salad:^000000 The lightness reduces the stickiness and brings out a satisfying bite.
Materials: ^4B00821 Sticky Flipper, 1 Green Herb, 1 Yellow Herb, 1 Light Sauce^000000
Cooking Method: Keep the sticky flipper soaked in ice water, and shred the green and yellow herbs.
           Take out an appropriate amount of flipper and mix it together with a suitable amount of the shredded herbs.
           When the herbs soften appropriately, add the light sauce and toss in the remaining herbs to mix.
           Refrigeration keeps the chewy texture and prevents it from sticking.

^0000FFTea of Ten Thousand Leaves:^000000 Enjoy it with cookies and cake.
Materials: ^4B00821 Blue Herb, 1 Red Herb, 1 Yellow Herb^000000
Cooking Method: Prepare well-dried and fermented herbs.
           In a pot that has been warmed only on the outside in boiling water so the inside does not get wet,
           add the herb leaves, pour in boiling water, close the lid, and steep for 2–3 minutes.
           It must not be too long or too short, and you may also add milk, lemon, and so on.

^008040Slippery Noodles:^000000 You need good chopstick skills to eat it easily.
Materials: ^4B00821 Grain, 1 Light Sauce, 1 Pumpkin Head, 1 Carrot^000000
Cooking Method: The basics of noodles is the dough. Grind grain with an appropriate protein content into flour,
          gradually add cold water to make dough, then stretch and fold repeatedly to form noodles.
          It is best to let the noodles rest for about a day.
          Boil the pumpkin head thoroughly in water, then pound it out and it will come out thin like noodles.
          Julienne the carrot and lightly stir-fry it, then boil the noodles in water and remove.
          At this time, when the water is about to boil over, splash in a little cold water,
          then boil once more and scoop them out.
          If you rub-wash the noodles in cold water, the chewy texture is exceptional.
          Put the noodle portion together with the pumpkin “noodles” into the hollowed pumpkin skin,
          pour in the prepared broth, and top with the stir-fried carrot.
          

^FF8040Seafood Dish:^000000 You could call it an assorted platter of various seafood.
Materials: ^4B00821 Clam Meat, 1 Spiky Gill, 1 Back Fin, 1 Tasty Fish^000000
Cooking Method: Lightly boil the fresh clam meat and tasty fish, then cut into appropriate sizes.
          Use tweezers or similar tools to remove all spines from the spiky gill,
          lightly boil it, then cut into appropriate sizes.
          Blanch the back fin in water, place the other ingredients on it,
          then fold it three times into a fan shape.

^400080Mixed Juice:^000000 An unexpected combination left an unexpected flavor. Lucky!
Materials: ^4B00821 Apple Juice, 1 Carrot Juice, 1 Grape Juice, 1 Orange Juice^000000
Cooking Method: Adjusting the ingredient ratios is important.
          Carrot juice becomes the base, and depending on the mixing order and ratios,
          the flavor combinations will differ. Those who value tanginess
          mix in the order of apple juice, orange juice, then grape juice,
          while those who value sweetness
          generally mix in the order of grape, apple, then orange.
          Some say there are even people who create and drink truly unexpected flavors.
          (It is said they “experienced a new world.”)
