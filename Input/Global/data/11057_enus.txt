^4D4DFFEasy and Simple Handmade Chocolate^000000

^46BEFF--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------^000000
Preparation: For White, 5 White Chocolates, 1 Milk
             For Black, 5 Chocolates, 1 Milk
^46BEFF--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------^000000

How to Make
Gift handmade chocolate is a simple dessert staple: melt the base couverture chocolate in a double boiler,
then pour and set it into the shape you want.

1. Break or chop the chocolate into small pieces so it melts well.
2. Melt the chocolate in a double boiler while maintaining a proper temperature.
3. Set aside part of the melted chocolate and add milk to make it into a smooth, creamy state.
4. Pour the rest into a mold of your desired shape, then cool it until it hardens completely.
5. Remove the hardened chocolate from the mold, spread the chocolate cream from step 3, and decorate to your taste.
* You may also mix in chopped nuts and spread them on top like a topping.


^CD3278Tangy-Sweet and Adorably Cute Strawberry Chocolate^000000

^46BEFF--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------^000000
Preparation: 2 fresh strawberries, 1 chocolate, 1 white chocolate

^46BEFF--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------^000000

How to Make
A cute, tangy-sweet chocolate dessert made by coating the surface of fresh strawberries with chocolate melted in a double boiler.
If you do not watch the moisture on the strawberries, the coating may not adhere well.

1. Finely chop the chocolate and melt it in a double boiler.
2. Hold the strawberry by the calyx and dip only about 70% of it to coat the surface with chocolate.
 -> First, remove any moisture so the strawberry surface is completely dry.
3. Skewer the coated strawberries and stand them upside down until the chocolate hardens.
4. Once the chocolate on the strawberry surface has fully set, decorate using the other melted chocolate color.



^4C1919A Deep Chocolate Feast: Chocolate Tart^000000

^46BEFF--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------^000000
Preparation: 3 Milk, 1 Egg, 1 Flour, 3 Chocolate

^46BEFF--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------^000000

A chocolate dessert made by filling a crisp-baked tart shell with smooth chocolate ganache, then letting it set.
You can enjoy chocolate’s soft yet deep flavor, making it great as a gift.

1. Add sifted flour and egg to softened room-temperature butter and make the dough.
2. Form a tart shell with the dough, then bake for about 15 minutes in an oven preheated to 180°C.
3. Chop the chocolate and melt it in a double boiler.
4. Add milk to the melted chocolate to make a smooth ganache.
5. Fill the tart shell baked in step 2 with the ganache from step 4, then let it set.

[Brownie]
^003A9D
Ingredients: Dark chocolate 130g, butter 75g, eggs 2, sugar 135g, a pinch of salt (omit if using salted butter)
             Unsweetened cocoa powder 15g, cake flour 100g (all-purpose flour is also OK), nuts as desired
^000000
Leave the eggs and butter at room temperature.

1. Melt the chocolate and butter in a double boiler.
2. Beat the eggs in a bowl, add salt and sugar, and stir.
   Stir until it becomes a soft ivory color and the sugar is completely dissolved.

3. Add step 1 to step 2 and whip.
4. Add sifted flour and cocoa powder to step 3 and mix well with a spatula.
   Stir gently so the batter does not collapse due to the egg foam.
   If you have prepared nuts, add them to the batter and mix.

Line an 18*18cm square pan with parchment paper, pour in the batter, tap it down a few times to remove air,
then bake for about 25 minutes in an oven preheated to 165~170°C.
If a skewer comes out with just a little batter clinging to it, it will be chewy.

[Pave Chocolate (Fresh Chocolate)]
^003A9D
Ingredients: Couverture chocolate 200g, fresh cream 80g, a little cocoa powder, butter 10g
^000000
    Adjust quantities by keeping the chocolate-to-cream ratio at 2:1 or 3:1.
    The higher the cream ratio, the lighter and softer the chocolate.
    You may also reduce the cream and add starch syrup.
    Take the butter out in advance and bring it to room temperature.

1. Bring the fresh cream to a boil over medium heat.
2. When it starts to boil, turn off the heat, add finely chopped chocolate and butter, and melt.
3. Pour the melted mixture from step 2 into a mold, let it cool, then refrigerate to set.
4. Take out the softly set chocolate and cut it into suitably sized pieces.
5. Roll it in cocoa powder to coat.



[Choco Snowball]
^003A9D
Ingredients: Dark chocolate 200g, butter 60g, sugar 70g, egg 1, cake flour 250g
             Baking powder 6g, powdered sugar, nuts 80g
^000000
1. Beat the room-temperature egg, add sugar, and stir well.
2. Melt the chocolate and butter in a double boiler.
3. Add step 2 to step 1 and mix well.
4. Add sifted flour and baking powder to step 3 and fold with the spatula, cutting from top to bottom.
   Move the spatula as if slicing to one side while rotating the bowl counterclockwise.
 -> This is to prevent excessive gluten formation.
5. Finely chop the prepared nuts, add them to the batter, and lightly mix with a spatula.
6. Put the batter into a plastic bag and let it rest in the refrigerator for about 30 minutes.
7. Take out the batter and pinch off small portions, shaping them into little egg-like balls.
8. Place them on a baking pan with space between and bake for about 15 minutes at 170~180°C.
9. Roll the baked choco balls in powdered sugar to dress them in white.
