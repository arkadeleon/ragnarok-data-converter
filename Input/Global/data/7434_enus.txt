^000088Synthesis Method for Elemental Potions that Increase Resistance^000000


----------------Foreword--------------------
While researching the four basic elements—fire, water, wind, and earth—
we discovered a catalyst that temporarily increases resistance to a specific element.
Using this, we record the synthesis method for elemental resistance potions in this book.
We hope it will be used more effectively when dealing with the four elements.
------------------------------------------


What is an ^FF0000Elemental Resist Potion^000000?

A potion that increases resistance to a specific element, reducing damage taken.
However, be aware that it will cause you to take increased damage
from the opposing element.




^0000FFResist Fire Potion^000000: A potion that increases resistance to the fire element.
Materials: ^4B00822 Lizard Necks, 1 Red Gemstone, 1 Empty Potion Bottle^000000
Manufacturing Method: Prepare a mortar.
          Place 2 lizard necks, catalysts of the fire element, into the mortar.
          Trigger a chemical reaction with the red gemstone and use Pharmacy
          to create the potion.
