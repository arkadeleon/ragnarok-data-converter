^000088The End-of-the-Century Kafra Legend^000000

This is an unbelievable tale—passed down like a legend since time immemorial within the Kafra Center.

In the mountain city of Payon, after the disappearance of Kafra service, monsters crossed the security line and broke into the village. Homes and rice fields lay devastated. In the very heart of town, only a handful of elderly people—too weak to even attempt evacuation—still kept their places. With the threat of beasts pouring out at any moment from Payon’s deep underground waterways, the worn and weary-faced elders had lost the will to live, sitting here and there with eyes half-closed, letting time pass.

“Mm…?”

A crone who had just begun to doze off opened her eyes at an unfamiliar presence. What she saw was a large figure wrapped deep in a black cloak. For a brief moment, beneath the cloak that didn’t quite hide the entire body, a white apron flashed into view—so the old woman rubbed her eyes over and over, trying to make sure.

“The Kafra ladies should all be gone… so why am I seeing an apron…?”

As she murmured to herself, another old man—eyes closed, trying to sleep—replied flatly.

“When you get old, you start seeing things. If you’ve got time for that, rest your eyes. Once the sun goes down, you’ll need them open.”
“Heh heh heh… I must really be getting up there.”

Forcing herself to ignore the unnatural sight before her, she closed her eyes again. The cloaked figure—whom she had assumed was a hallucination—strode toward the village’s one and only remaining tavern. Soon the door creaked open, and a man slumped sideways in his seat, half-soaked in drink, muttered toward the sound.

“There’s nothing left to sell here… unless you want a drink.”
“…”

The newcomer stayed silent for a beat, then walked straight up to him.

“Are you the owner here?”

As if he couldn’t be bothered to answer, the man kept his eyes closed and pointed at the shelf.

“If you came for liquor, what’s left in that shelf is all there is. Can’t sell it anyway, so do what you want.”

At his words, the cloaked figure stepped to the shelf, took the bottle that seemed the fullest, then sat beside the drowsy man and raised it to their mouth. With a loud gulp, the harsh liquor went down, followed by a brief sound of catching breath.

“Owner… why has this village come to this?”

At the gravelly voice, the man’s sleepiness seemed to fade; he opened his eyes and spoke.

“You’re asking because you don’t know? You must’ve been away for a long time.”

He glanced at the bottle-holder’s face, then slowly got to his feet.

“Hiding your face that deep… sounds like you’ve got a story. Drinking raw liquor’s bad for you. Want some cheese?”
“Gladly…”

The man set down a chunk of cheese, and the cloaked figure reached out to take it. The hand that emerged from the cloak was rough and massive.

“After all the Kafras vanished, the barrier that used to block off the boundary between the dungeon and the town got damaged. So when night comes and the monsters start moving, they spill into the village. Yesterday, they took an old woman.”

From beneath the cloak came a gloomy voice.

“So there are no Kafras left here?”
The man snorted.

“Only here? In Prontera, Morroc, Geffen, Alberta— even at headquarters in Aldebaran, you can’t find the old Kafra services anymore. Sometimes a mobile device called Kafra GT shows up, and it squeezes taxes out of us as a ‘town boundary fee.’ Back when we still paid, it at least pretended to stand watch in front of the dungeon. These days, you can’t even spot it unless it’s here to shake you down. Come to think of it… it’s about time it showed up.”

The cloaked figure said:

“In that situation, even if the old Kafras returned, it wouldn’t amount to much.”

The man answered with another snort.

“See those elders guarding the village entrance? Those old fools are still living on a pointless belief that the Kafras will come back. But even if they did, nothing would change. All that’s left are old people waiting to die— and I’m the only one here who can work.”

He spoke with open contempt, paused to breathe, then continued.

“Even if the Kafra ladies came back, what would it change—damn old fools.”
“…”

Without a word, the cloaked figure took another drink. How much time passed in that silence? Then a mechanical voice sounded from outside the tavern.

– Beep… Karl Basten. 76 years old. Male. Identity verification complete. Outstanding unpaid regular taxes confirmed. You are requested to accompany us to headquarters.

It was the voice of Kafra GT. The tavern owner’s face twisted slightly.

“Old man didn’t pay his taxes, huh… Should’ve paid on time. Tsk.”

He silently filled a cup with liquor. The cloaked figure asked:

“What happens to someone who doesn’t pay?”
“What happens? They get forcibly taken to headquarters and sent to a labor camp, or they get their house seized. One or the other.”
“And you have no intention of helping him?”
“If one or two old men disappear, do you think it changes anything?”

As if his mood had turned foul, he gulped down his drink. Outside, Kafra GT’s voice continued.

– Karl Basten. If you do not comply, forced execution will be unavoidable.

Right after the announcement, a loud clang of scrap metal crashing rang out—likely the old man throwing something at Kafra GT.

– Karl Basten. Attacking Kafra GT is a serious offense. This device is state property. If you do not comply, you may be shot on the spot.

It seemed he still refused.

– Karl Basten. Drop the weapon in your hand. This device is state property. If you do not comply, you will be shot.

When the mechanical voice reached that point, the tavern owner slammed his cup onto the table and shouted.

“I’ve listened long enough— that pile of scrap is getting arrogant!!”

He grabbed the long gun hanging on the wall and ran out. The cloaked figure only kept drinking in silence, watching his back. The owner loaded a round, aimed at Kafra GT, and yelled:

“State property, my ass!! Since when did you start doing business while babbling about the state?!”

– Beep… Charles Raven. 38 years old. Male. Identity verification complete. Armed: 27mm shotgun. Armor penetration: 0.001%. Critical damage probability: zero. Enemy force comparison complete. Disarm immediately and kneel on the ground, Charles Raven.

“Crazy bastard…”

With the mutter, the shotgun roared. Startled by the blast, the elderly screamed, ran into their homes, and locked their doors. The shotgun—nearly useless in penetration—had no effect on Kafra GT’s thick armor. A faint ticking of fragments followed, and Kafra GT’s IFF lamp turned red.

– Armor breach: 0. Damage: 0. Returning fire.

Kafra GT’s massive Gatling gun completed its firing prep. Then a heavy burst poured out.

‘Dudududududu…’

It wasn’t fast—only three rounds per second—but the power was anything but weak. Raven zigzagged to avoid the storm of bullets and fired at Kafra GT’s faceplate. But the faceplate, protected with an Ernium alloy surface, took no damage at all. Raven rolled down beneath a slope where Kafra GT would have trouble identifying him.

“A machine like that can’t chase me down a downhill slope—”

But his hope twisted into something else. Kafra GT put the pursuit mission on standby and executed the next mission.

– The entire village is deemed hostile and noncompliant with the Kafra Center. Requesting authorization to incinerate all buildings within the village.

It was requesting permission from Kafra headquarters to burn the town to the ground. Rage ignited in Raven’s eyes. Forgetting his plan to evade, he charged back up the hill.

“Who gave you permission to burn our village?!”

As if authorization had been granted, a flamethrower nozzle deployed beneath Kafra GT’s Gatling assembly. A small pilot flame flickered, ready to become an inferno at any moment. Realizing the elders had fled inside due to the firefight, Raven shouted at the top of his lungs.

“There are old people inside those houses! You insane bastard!”

– Authorization from Kafra headquarters received. Proceeding to incinerate the entire village as scheduled. First target: the home of Karl Basten, who refused cooperation with tax collection.

Raven grabbed a large rock from the ground and hurled it at Kafra GT’s head. With a loud clang, Kafra GT’s head tilted sharply—yet it seemed unconcerned. After a brief beeping adjustment, it turned and spewed flames toward an eastern shack.

“No!!!!—”

Despite Raven’s scream, the fire from the flamethrower wrapped the house in an instant. He tensed to rush in and save the old man trapped inside—at that exact moment, Kafra GT’s barrel swung toward Raven.

– Do not move, Charles Raven. If you move even a toe, you will be treated as obstructing Kafra enforcement and will be dealt with accordingly.

The Gatling gun was aimed at Raven’s forehead with not even a hair’s breadth of error. Every pore in Raven’s body felt ready to explode with fury—yet before Kafra GT, he was only a weak human being, forced to submit to a machine called Kafra GT.

And then—

“…”
– Beep!

From within the flames, a figure in a deeply pulled black cloak emerged, walking out while carrying the trapped elder to safety.

“Who are you?!”

Raven realized it was the traveler who had been drinking with him inside his tavern. Kafra GT, sensing the appearance of a new enemy, activated its IFF detection.

– Beep… Name: unknown. Sex: unknown. Age: unknown. A new entity not registered in the database. Kafra Center Command requests a search for information on an unidentified individual, estimated height 210 cm or more, estimated weight 109 kg.

While GT busily moved to identify the target, the cloaked figure laid the elder—scorched in places—down atop a nearby grain sack. Then the figure spoke to Raven.

“Fortunately, he isn’t badly hurt. Take him somewhere safe….”

Raven hurried over and supported the elder’s shoulder. Kafra GT redirected the barrel that had been aimed at Raven, now pointing it at the cloaked figure.

– State your name, sex, and age. If you refuse, you will be deemed a hostile force and I will switch to combat mode.

The cloaked figure began walking slowly toward Kafra GT.

“My age is twenty-four…”

– Beep… received.

With each step, the figure continued revealing their identity.

“My sex is female, and… long ago, I was called by this name.”

The black cloak wrapped tightly around the head slipped free.
Broad shoulders—delicately yet powerfully sculpted with muscle—gleamed bronze under the harsh sunlight.

From Raven—still staring—and from the elders peeking out through windows, a single name burst out without anyone needing to prompt it.

“Y-you are…!!”
“Kafra… Kafra Tailing!!”
“Has Kafra truly returned?!”

Kafra GT’s IFF lamp emitted an intensely strong red glow.

– Primary Target. Threat Level 10. Kafra Tailing detected!! Requesting emergency support from Comma—

But Kafra GT’s transmission never finished.

“KWAAANG!!—”

With a thunderous crash, Kafra Tailing’s massive fist smashed into Kafra GT’s faceplate. The towering Kafra GT—well over 2.5 meters—lifted about 30 centimeters off the ground as it was hurled backward. Barely stabilizing itself, Kafra GT sent an emergency signal to headquarters from where it had fallen.

– Bee-bee-beep… Threat Level 10. Threat Level 10. Emergency supp—

Kafra GT was a machine—incapable of fear.
And yet, for some reason, its metal body was trembling in fine spasms.

Kafra Tailing walked up slowly and looked down at it.

“What I did just now… was my anger, after living nameless because of you.”

A deep shadow fell across GT’s faceplate.

“And this… is the weight of the blood and tears my sisters shed.”

With the sound of metal shattering, Tailing’s leg drove through GT’s chest. The 70mm Ernium top plate encasing its torso exploded into fragments like a peanut shell cracking apart, scattering everywhere. Kafra GT spoke rapidly.

– The main force will arrive here soon… Even if you defeat me, it’s already too late…

As if mocking that, Kafra Tailing snorted.

“Kafra GT. Transmit my words to headquarters exactly as I say them…”

GT increased the sensitivity of its voice-recognition sensor. And then Tailing whispered low into its “ear.”

“Abandon the happy delusion that I’m the only Kafra still alive.”

Her whisper quietly spread through Kafra headquarters as an internal broadcast.

“To bring you down… one finger is enough.”

Kafra GT’s IFF sensor shook like it had gone mad. Kafra Tailing’s finger moved slowly toward GT’s power switch.

And the switch flipped to OFF.

– Whiiiiiiir…

The sound of electric motors driving ceased completely. Tailing turned her head back toward Raven and asked:

“Which way is Aldebaran?”

Raven, barely holding back something swelling in his chest, pointed northwest.

“We’ll meet soon… my sisters.”
