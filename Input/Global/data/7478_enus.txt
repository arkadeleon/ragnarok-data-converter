^FF0000Whole Barbecue:^000000 Don’t worry—I’m not grilling the “barrel” itself!
Materials: ^4B00821 Meat, 1 Ember, 1 Coal, 1 Spicy Sauce, 1 Wood Piece, 1 White Herb^000000
Cooking Method: Wash the meat thoroughly inside and out, then spread spicy sauce evenly over it.
          Finely chop the white herb and sprinkle just a little, then place the ember on the wood piece
          and prepare a steady, gentle fire so it won’t go out, and grill the meat.
          While grilling, spread spicy sauce evenly from time to time,
          then sprinkle with finely chopped herbs when serving.

^800000Soul Hunt Bread:^000000 Watch out—your soul might fly away!
Materials: ^4B00821 Bread, 1 Mastela Fruit, 1 Sweet Sauce, 1 Ment, 1 Half-Soul, 1 Unripe Apple^000000
Cooking Method: Peel the Mastela fruit and apple, then dice them.
         Wash the ment clean, extract its juice,
         and marinate the diced Mastela fruit and apple with sugar for 30 minutes.
         (A roughly 1:1 sugar ratio.)
         Bring the marinated mixture to a boil over high heat; when foam rises, reduce to medium heat,
         add a bit more sugar, and simmer gently.
         (Keep stirring so it doesn’t stick to the bottom.)
         Reduce until it becomes clear and glossy to make jam.
         Grind the half-soul well and age it together with sweet sauce.
         Spread the jam and sweet sauce onto the bread.

^008040Grilled Alligator Vegetable Steam:^000000 Surprisingly tender and chewy!
Materials: ^4B00821 Anolian Skin, 1 Pumpkin Head, 1 Carrot, 1 Aloe Leaf,
         1 Yellow Herb, 1 Yellow Spice^000000
Cooking Method: Wash the Anolian skin clean, mince it finely, then add salt, pepper, egg, water, and yellow spice
          and knead until sticky and elastic.
          Julienne the carrot lengthwise and blanch it.
          Blanch the aloe leaf and yellow herb, then squeeze out the moisture firmly.
          Shred the pumpkin head, lightly stir-fry it, and let it cool.
          Lay a cloth on a cutting board, place the Anolian skin mixture on top,
          steam it in a preheated steamer for about 15 minutes,
          then cut into bite-sized pieces and serve with the vegetables.

^FF8040Special Mountain-and-Sea Delicacies:^000000 To catch a boar, go to the mountains; to fish, go to the sea!
Materials: ^4B00821 Fish Tail, 1 Aloe Leaf, 1 Boar Mane Hair, 1 Tasty Fish,
         1 Pet Food, 1 Sweet Sauce^000000
Cooking Method: Lightly blanch the aloe leaf in salt water and set aside.
          Remove scales from the tasty fish, coat it with sweet sauce, and marinate for 10 minutes.
          Wrap the marinated fish with aloe leaf, steam it together with the fish tail for 10 minutes,
          then plate.
          Cut the boar mane hair to about 4 cm and blanch lightly.
          Cut the pet food into bite-sized pieces, coat with sweet sauce while grilling it in a pan.
         

^400080Skewer Grill:^000000 If you don’t want to get pricked while eating, trim the tips of the spikes!
Materials: ^4B00821 Scaly Skin, 1 Spiky Gill, 1 Soft Leaf, 1 Meat,
         1 Edible Mushroom, 1 Clam Meat^000000
Cooking Method: Cut the soft leaf into 4 cm squares, and slice the mushrooms lengthwise along their shape.
          Wash the scaly skin clean and set aside,
          and select and prepare long spikes from the spiky gill.
          Lightly boil the meat, and lightly blanch the clam meat.
          Thread in the order of meat, vegetables, then clam meat onto the long spikes,
          heat the scaly skin on the grill, then grill the skewers on top of it.
