Monthly Alchemist (hereafter “Alche”): Today’s guests are the Bar brothers, the developers of the legendary SP-increasing potion that has recently become a hot topic among Alchemists.
First, could you please introduce yourselves?

Bain: I am Bain, the eldest of the Bar brothers. My hobby is collecting flowers.

Bazin: I am Bazin, the younger of the Bar brothers. My hobby is puppet theater. My brother and I were selected as two of the Top 10 Alchemists this year.
Thank you all for your continued support.

Alche: The Dream SP Increase Potion series has been drawing a lot of attention lately.
We heard it is a collaborative work by the two of you. May we ask how it came to be?

Bain: Yes. At first, my brother and I were researching a stone called the Stone of Life.
However, making a living became difficult, and we eventually ended up producing and selling potions that customers wanted.
In the beginning, it was merely refining grape juice for SP recovery to slightly improve its effectiveness.
But while combining various SP recovery items, we discovered that it was possible to refine a large-capacity recovery potion that not only far surpassed existing SP recovery items, but also increased the maximum SP amount.
Of course, we have no intention of monopolizing the method of creating such potions.

Bazin: This compounding technique requires extreme care and a great deal of time.
Even if one were to discover the required materials and methods, it would not be easy to produce.
Additionally, the success rate is very low, so mistakes may simply result in wasted materials.
We hope that young Alchemists with little experience will not suffer major losses by failing large-scale refinements of SP increase potions.

Alche: So what you are saying is that the key lies not so much in the materials or recipe itself, but in the skill and aptitude of the person creating it?

Bain: Exactly. Even with the same ingredients and seasoning, the taste of a dish can be sweet, bitter, or spicy depending on who prepares it.
The total number of refinement methods we have tested so far for SP increase potions amounts to an astonishing 136,072.
Even at 100 refinements per day, it took nearly four years.
Therefore, anyone who believes they can find a better method than ours is welcome to try.

Alche: Nevertheless, you are willing to publicly release this method for the development and contribution of Alchemists as a whole.
As an exclusive feature, this is a tremendous honor. Thank you very much.

Bazin: Not at all. We hope that this will further energize the activities of Alchemists and contribute to maintaining peace in the world.
Even if we tried to monopolize this method for profit, we believe it would not last long.

Alche: Is that so? Why do you think that?

Bain: As my brother said, there are currently almost no potions that increase maximum SP.
Until now, SP has mainly been increased forcibly through equipment, card options, or special skills.

However, merely increasing the maximum SP capacity does not solve the problem.
What we increase is the size of the engine, not the fuel itself.

Bazin: Of course, SP increase potions do have some recovery effect.
However, that is simply the inherent effect of the materials themselves, and they do not produce anything beyond that.
Ultimately, what we do is temporarily assist the activation of the mind and body, allowing more mana to be stored within the body.
This shares common ground with the techniques developed by martial monks who have trained relentlessly in recent years.
Though the approach differs, the root is the same.

Bain: Therefore, even if we alone possessed this knowledge, the only ones who would lose out are Alchemists themselves.
By releasing this refinement method, the Alchemist community as a whole will gain great benefits.
The value and rarity of the materials used in each method will also increase.
However, if only we know the methods and materials, wealth will circulate only between us and consumers, producing no additional ripple effects.
Even if we were to obtain large-scale factories or sponsors to mass-produce these potions, the sudden increase in demand for existing materials would undoubtedly create market distortion.
The amount of items used by Alchemists as potion materials is always limited in the market.

Bazin: That’s right. Even if we tried to disguise it by purchasing large quantities of other items, the truth would eventually come out.
Such actions would only make things difficult in the long run.

Alche: I understand your perspective.
In the end, secrecy brings only temporary gains, whereas market activation and increased trade among Alchemists promise far greater benefits.

Bain: Yes, we would appreciate it if you saw it that way.
Even if what we are doing now does not bring much profit, we believe that actions taken to help others or to serve a good cause eventually return to oneself.
This is what we call the "Golden Rule of Life."

Alche: Oh! So what an Alchemist who creates gold ultimately discovers is the "Golden Rule of Life"!

Bain: (Laughs) It’s nothing that grand. Saying it out loud makes me feel a bit embarrassed.

Alche: Still, thanks to the two of you, many people can now easily make use of new potions.
We have no doubt that this decision will serve as a great example for many Alchemists.
What are your future plans?

Bain: My brother and I intend to continue devoting the rest of our lives to researching the "Stone of Life."
That was our original goal.
We also hope that the potions we create will be put to meaningful use in many ways.

Alche: Thank you.
Lastly, our magazine would like to introduce individuals interested in investing research funds to support the continued studies of the Bain and Bazin brothers.
Those who wish to know more details are encouraged to contact the Monthly Alchemist editorial department or consult the research management officer at a nearby Alchemist Guild.

With that, we conclude today’s interview. Thank you very much.

Bain & Bazin: Thank you.

(Attached below is the recipe information for the SP increase potion.)

Recipe

- SP Increase Potion (Small): Empty Bottle 10 + Lemon 10 + Grape 10 + Sweet Sauce 1
- SP Increase Potion (Medium): Empty Bottle 10 + Honey 10 + Blue Herb 10 + Sweet Sauce 1
- SP Increase Potion (Large): Empty Bottle 10 + Royal Jelly 10 + Blue Herb 15 + Sweet Sauce 1

Effect

For approximately 500 seconds, increases the user’s MSP by up to 1% ~ 20%.
The larger the size, the greater the effect.
