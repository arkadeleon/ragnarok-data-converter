^FF0000Stir-Fried Grasshopper:^000000 Don’t worry—it’s already stir-fried, so it won’t jump around in your mouth.
Materials: ^4B00821 Grasshopper Hind Leg, 1 Cooking Oil^000000
Cooking Method: Heat a frying pan over medium heat.
           (With a new frying pan, it takes time for the oil to soak into the bottom,
           so some people use fatty parts like Savage lard to season it..)
           Once it is heated properly, add 2 tablespoons of cooking oil,
           then add 1 hind leg and lightly stir-fry.
           When it turns slightly golden, add the remaining 1 and add 1 more tablespoon of oil,
           then fry until crispy.

^0000FFGrape Juice Herb Tea:^000000 Just grind grapes into a potion and you’re good!
Materials: ^4B00821 Grape, 1 Red Potion^000000
Cooking Method: Put the grape and one serving of red potion into a blender and grind to make juice.
           The key point is to grind the skin and seeds together!
           When it is blended enough, add the remaining serving and mix for 10 seconds.



^800000Honey Grape Juice:^000000 Add strawberries too, and it’s the icing on the cake!
Materials: ^4B00821 Strawberry, 1 Grape, 1 Red Herb^000000
Cooking Method: Made almost the same way as Grape Juice Herb Tea,
           but the key is to add sweet strawberries instead of the final potion serving!

^008040Frog Spawn Soup:^000000 Soup served with sauce
Materials: ^4B00821 Grain, 1 Frog Spawn^000000
Cooking Method: Finely grind the grain and set it aside, and soak the frog spawn in ice water to prepare it.
           (The reason for soaking in ice water is to keep a chewier texture.)
           Add the finely ground grain powder to an appropriate amount of water and simmer over low heat.
           When it thickens,
           add the frog spawn that was kept in ice water.
           When the frog spawn is lightly cooked, add the sauce, bring it to a brief boil, then serve.
          
^FF8040Steamed Crab Claw:^000000 It’s fun to pick and eat!
Materials: ^4B00821 Claw, 1 Green Herb, 1 Red Potion^000000
Cooking Method: First, marinate the claw well in red potion a day in advance.
           Line a steamer with a cloth, spread green herbs on top,
           then place the well-seasoned claw on top and steam it.

^400080Stir-Fried Monkey Tail:^000000 It seems like it would be tough, but it really isn’t.
Materials: ^4B00821 Monkey Tail, 1 Cooking Oil^000000
Cooking Method: Peel the monkey tail and cut it into bite-sized pieces.
           Heat a frying pan over medium heat, add cooking oil, and stir-fry an appropriate amount.
           If you fry too much at once, the meat becomes tough, so fry in moderate portions.
