^000088Episode I – Rune-Midgarts Kingdom^000000

Holy Rune — ^880000the Kingdom of Midgard^000000 is a hereditary monarchy that has endured for over a thousand years.
Centered around its capital, Prontera, the kingdom has formed a vast realm and has long existed upon the continent of Midgard.
Currently ruled by King Tristan III, the Rune-Midgarts Kingdom venerates the chief god Odin and follows a polytheistic faith.

A grand cathedral dedicated to Odin stands in the capital, Prontera.
Located at the very heart of the continent, the city is renowned as a bustling hub of commerce and trade, with countless travelers passing through each day.
In the past, Prontera was home to the Swordman Training Grounds, where many aspiring knights gathered to pursue their dreams.
However, its former prestige has since been passed on to the satellite city of Izlude, and Prontera has now become a city devoted to Knights and Crusaders.

To the northwest lies the magical city of Geffen, a place shrouded in mystery due to its connection to another arcane city, Geffenia, where ancient elven treasures are said to slumber.
Aspiring Magicians frequently visit Geffen, and the towering Geffen Tower at the center of the city has become both its symbol and a sacred site for mages.

In the southwest stands the desert city of Morroc, a harsh and desolate land.
As a result, few travelers pass through, yet the city is home to the Thief Guild, drawing many aspiring thieves.
Rumors also speak of the Assassin Guild, known as the “Fangs of the Desert,” hidden somewhere within the Sograt Desert near Morroc.
Because of these whispers, countless assassin hopefuls continue to traverse the desert in search of the guild.

The mountainous city of Payon, located to the northeast, is famous as the village of Archers.
True to its rugged terrain, the city has produced generations of hunters, leading to the development of exceptional bow-making and archery techniques.
Their marksmanship is said to be nearly flawless, and the powerful composite bow known as the “Gakgung” has become Payon’s signature specialty.

To the southeast lies the commercial city of Alberta, a city that prospered through maritime trade.
However, the route to Alberta is complex, causing many travelers to choose the sea route via the satellite city of Izlude rather than traveling overland.
Though pirate attacks are not uncommon, those who overcome such dangers and arrive safely are sure to fall in love with Alberta’s lively atmosphere and free-spirited marketplace.
The Alberta Merchant Guild, which aims to control 30% of the continent’s trade, is headquartered here, attracting many aspiring Merchants eager to dominate the market.

The tranquil city of Aldebaran is best known for the Clock Tower that stands at its center.
Once thriving due to its clock-making industry, Aldebaran saw countless clocks abandoned by engineers during production.
These discarded clocks eventually occupied the Clock Tower, where they began to function, live, and even multiply on their own.

As a result, an unintended dungeon was born at the heart of the city.
Many adventurers, drawn by rumors, venture inside to explore the Clock Tower and engage in battle.
Yet aside from the few who manage to return alive, the interior remains an unsolved labyrinth to this day.
