^FF0000Herb-Marinated Galbi:^000000 Premium ribs aged with herb-infused marinade—tenderness at its finest!
Materials: ^4B00821 Meat, 1 Red Herb, 1 Yellow Herb, 1 Green Herb, 1 Sweet Sauce, 1 Lemon^000000
Cooking Method: Trim off excess fat, score the meat, then soak it in water for about 1 hour to remove blood.
          Finely chop the herbs, mix an appropriate amount of sweet sauce and water, boil for about 1 hour,
          and once it cools, add lemon juice, stir well, and chill.
          Prepare a wide bowl and a container; pour the marinade into the wide bowl,
          dip and coat each piece of well-prepared meat, then stack them neatly into the container.
          Pour the remaining marinade into the container afterward, let it age for half a day to a full day,
          then grill.

^0000FFMorroc Fruit Wine:^000000 Sweet and tangy! OK even for your palate if you hate alcohol!
Materials: ^4B00821 Strawberry, 1 Lemon, 1 Orange, 1 Alcohol, 1 Grape^000000
Cooking Method: Use a squeezer (a tool for extracting fruit juice) to thoroughly juice the lemon and orange.
          Remove the stems from cleanly washed strawberries, peel the grapes and remove the seeds,
          then put them in a blender and mix for 30 seconds.
          Add 1 serving of alcohol and lemon juice, then mix for 5 more seconds.
          Finally, add 1 alcohol and orange juice, then mix for 15 seconds to finish!
  
^800000Cream Sandwich:^000000 Loaded with cream! There’s too much sandwich cream—please don’t put it on your head!
Materials: ^4B00821 Bread, 1 Milk, 1 Cheese, 1 Yellow Herb, 1 Meat, 1 Sweet Sauce^000000
Cooking Method: Lightly toast the bread in butter, and whip the milk to make cream (crema).
          Cut the meat patty thick and cook it to the right doneness.
          Place herbs on the bread, spread sweet sauce, then add the meat patty.
          Add cheese, then the remaining herbs, pile on plenty of cream, and cover with the other slice of bread.

^008040Lutie Spicy Mixed Cold Noodles:^000000 Low price and a dizzying spicy kick!
Materials: ^4B00821 Grain, 1 Spicy Sauce, 1 Ice Piece, 1 Carrot, 1 Pumpkin Head^000000
Cooking Method: Gradually add hot water to flour made by grinding grain with an appropriate protein content
          to make a half-kneaded dough, then keep kneading until it becomes elastic and glossy.
          Thinly knead the dough and put it into a square mold (with mesh fixed at the bottom), cover it,
          press down firmly from above to extrude noodles,
          boil them in boiling water, rinse by rubbing in cold water, and drain in a strainer.
          Boil the pumpkin head thoroughly in water, then pound it out and it will come out thin like noodles.
          Julienne the carrot thinly.
          Put the noodles and pumpkin noodles in a bowl, add ice,
          dress with an appropriate amount of spicy sauce on top, and garnish with carrot.

^FF8040Seasoned Jellyfish:^000000 It is cooked thoroughly, so there is no worry of being stung.
Materials: ^4B00821 Tentacle, 1 White Herb, 1 Soft Leaf, 1 Old Frying Pan, 1 Ink^000000
Cooking Method: Rinse the soft leaf in water, squeeze out the moisture well,
          and season/toss it with ink sauce.
          Shred and wash the tentacle, heat water in an old frying pan and blanch it lightly,
          then rinse in cold water and squeeze out the moisture well.
          If blanched too long, it shrinks, so dip it briefly in warm water and remove.
          Mix the shredded white herb, tentacle, and seasoned leaves together,
          add a little more ink seasoning, chill well, and plate.

^400080Ancient Fish Dish:^000000 Don’t worry—it won’t feel like kissing an ancient fish at all!
Materials: ^4B00821 Ancient Fish Lips, 1 Raccoon Leaf, 1 White Herb, 1 Tasty Fish, 1 Sweet Sauce^000000
Cooking Method: Wash the ancient fish lips clean in salt water.
          Remove the head and guts from the tasty fish, then fillet it into three pieces along the backbone,
          remove small bones with tweezers, and marinate it using one sweet sauce.
          Once sufficiently marinated, line the bottom of a steamer with raccoon leaves and steam.
          At this time, place it with the scales facing down so the flesh does not shrink.
          Before steam rises in the steamer, drizzle sweet sauce evenly,
          sprinkle shredded white herb on top, and steam for about 20 minutes.
