----------------Foreword--------------------
The alchemy of life is an extremely difficult endeavor.
The first step we approached in order to study the high-level task known as life synthesis
was the synthesis of plants.
However, the weak life force of ordinary plants brought nothing but failure.
With no research results in sight and just as we were about to give up,
we learned of the existence of man-eating plants.
Plants with stronger vitality and a ferocious nature compared to others...
The man-eating plants we discovered while wandering the Mjolnir Mountains and areas near Juno
brought us great achievements in our research and immense joy.
It was the moment we opened our eyes to the astonishing world of plant synthesis.
------------------------------------------




What is a ^FF8040Plant Bottle^000000?

When thrown onto the ground, a man-eating plant instantly grows
and attacks creatures that come near it in order to devour them.
This plant is preserved in a bottle through a special processing method.
However, only alchemists who have mastered specialized techniques
are said to be able to use it.


Materials: ^4B00822 Man-Eating Plant Flowers, 1 Empty Bottle^000000
Manufacturing Method: Prepare a mortar.
          Place 2 man-eating plant flowers into the mortar, and while carrying this book,
          use Pharmacy to bottle the result into an empty bottle.
          The alchemy used to summon man-eating plants must be learned separately
          from standard synthesis techniques in order to be used.
