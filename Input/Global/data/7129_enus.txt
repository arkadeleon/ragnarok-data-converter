^000088Alchemy Basics for Manufacturing Hydrochloric Acid Bottles^000000


----------------Foreword--------------------

While researching the never-stopping heart, we discovered that under certain conditions,
hydrogen chloride is generated.
Using this, we record here the method for creating hydrochloric acid.
Hydrochloric acid is a strong acidic solution due to its high degree of ionization.
At times, it may become strong enough to dissolve stone, so please handle it with caution.

------------------------------------------


What is ^008040Hydrochloric Acid^000000?

A solution made by dissolving hydrogen chloride (HCl) in water.
When this solution is stored in a bottle, it becomes a hydrochloric acid bottle.



Materials: ^4B00821 Never-Stopping Heart, 1 Empty Bottle^000000
Manufacturing Method: Prepare a mortar.
                    Place the never-stopping heart into the mortar, add a small amount of water, and heat it using a water bath.
                    Maintain the water temperature at 72 degrees.
                    Distill the gas generated at this time to obtain hydrogen chloride.
                    Fill an empty bottle with water and allow the hydrogen chloride to dissolve into the water.
                    When 100 hydrogen chloride molecules dissolve and equilibrium is reached,
                    most of the molecules dissociate, creating a strong hydrochloric acid bottle.
                    For easier manufacturing, carry this book, prepare the materials,
                    and use Pharmacy, the basic skill of alchemy.
