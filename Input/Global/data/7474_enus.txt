^FF0000Bomber Steak:^000000 Explosively spicy. It brings your appetite back to life!
Materials: ^4B00821 Meat, 1 Old Frying Pan, 1 Green Herb, 1 Red Chili, 1 Spicy Sauce^000000
Cooking Method: Tenderize the prepared meat portion by pounding it with a mace or similar tool.
          Grind well-dried green herbs and chili, and season the meat in advance.
          Heat the pan, first add cooking oil, melt butter, and grill the meat while sprinkling salt.
          Salt should be added right before grilling so it does not become tough and tastes better.
          When one side is cooked, season with salt, flip only once, and grill until it browns.
          Place the meat on a plate, garnish with 3–4 fresh green herb leaves, and drizzle spicy sauce.
          

^0000FFHerb Honey Tea:^000000 Recommended for you who enjoy a subtle aroma!
Materials: ^4B00821 Honey, 1 White Herb, 1 Yellow Spice^000000
Cooking Method: The rule for herb tea is to use dried herbs,
          but it is also fine to use fresh herbs (raw leaves).
          However, keep in mind that herbs are plants that contain over 90% moisture.
          If using fresh herbs, you need to use about 3–4 times the amount of dried herbs
          to properly enjoy the aroma. (You can also reduce the amount and use yellow spice.)
          Put an appropriate amount into a teapot and pour in hot water to steep.
          Do not forget to close the lid so the aroma does not escape.
          Steeping time varies—fresh herbs 4–5 minutes,
          dried herbs around 3 minutes—find the time that suits you.
          Add a little yellow spice to enhance the fragrance, and add honey for sweetness.


^800000Fruits Mix:^000000 Crunchy-crunchy. A festival of fruits!
Materials: ^4B00821 Apple, 1 Banana, 1 Orange, 1 Strawberry, 1 Yellow Spice^000000
Cooking Method: Wash the apple, orange, and strawberries thoroughly.
          Peel the apple, orange, and banana, then cut into easy-to-eat pieces,
          and slice the strawberries in half lengthwise.
          In a deep glass, it is best to place firm apples and oranges at the bottom,
          and bananas and strawberries on top, but the mixing method is up to you.
          Drizzle spiced syrup over the fruit in the glass.
         
^008040Tentacle Cheese Gratin:^000000 Lots of cheese over slick tentacles!
Materials: ^4B00821 Tentacle, 1 Cheese, 1 Chestnut Sweet Potato, 1 Sweet Sauce^000000
Cooking Method: Peel the sweet potato, cut into large cubes, and boil.
          Wash the tentacle clean and cut into 3–4 cm lengths.
          Heat a pan until hot, add oil, then add the tentacle
          and lightly stir-fry while seasoning with salt and pepper.
          Butter a gratin dish, add the stir-fried tentacle and boiled sweet potato,
          pour in plenty of sweet sauce, top generously with cheese, and bake in the oven for about 10 minutes.

^FF8040Clam Soup:^000000 Goodbye, refreshing clam soup! Now it’s sweet clam soup!
Materials: ^4B00821 Clam Meat, 1 Conch, 1 Honey, 1 Sweet Sauce^000000
Cooking Method: Wash the conch clean, boil it, remove the dark-blue innards,
          and cut into appropriate sizes.
          When foam forms while boiling, skim it off, then strain the broth through fine cloth.
          Add the conch and scrub-washed clams to the strained broth and boil again,
          then season with honey and sweet sauce.
        

^400080Taste Tang:^000000 Soft inside, sweet and crispy outside!
Materials: ^4B00821 Sweet Potato, 1 Chestnut Sweet Potato, 1 Roasted Sweet Potato, 1 Sweet Sauce^000000
Cooking Method: Wash the sweet potato clean and cut it into cubes with the skin on.
          At this time, gulp down the roasted sweet potato.
          Prepare enough oil to submerge the sweet potatoes,
          and when the oil heats up, add all the prepared sweet potatoes
          and fry until yellow or brown.
          Transfer the properly colored sweet potatoes to a plate to drain oil,
          then add 1 tablespoon of cooking oil and 1 serving of sweet sauce to the pan and mix.
          Add the sweet potatoes to the pan and coat evenly with sweet syrup,
          then simmer until crispy.
          After plating, sprinkle black sesame seeds to double the nuttiness.
