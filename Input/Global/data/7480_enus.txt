^FF0000Tendon Sauté:^000000 Remember—there is tender tendon too!
Materials: ^4B00821 Tendon, 1 Pot, 1 Cheese, 1 Spicy Sauce, 1 Coal, 1 Green Herb, 1 Ink^000000 
Cooking Method: Cut the tendon into 3 cm pieces and season lightly with salt and pepper.
          Blanch part of the green herb briefly in boiling water, cool it in cold water, and chop the rest.
          Put ink and water into a pot and cook, then add spicy sauce to make a thick sauce.
          Add the cooled herbs and cheese and sauté the tendon until nicely browned.
          At this time, turn the coal into charcoal fire and cook it gently over the ember heat.
          Place the cooked tendon in a bowl, spoon the prepared sauce on top, then sprinkle chopped green herb.
          
^0000FFTristan 12-Year Vintage:^000000 A fruit wine with a whisky-like feel!
Materials: ^4B00821 Yggdrasil Leaf, 1 Orange, 1 Alcohol, 1 Royal Jelly, 1 Blue Potion,
         1 Yellow Spice, 1 Aloe Leaf^000000
Cooking Method: Juice the orange using a squeezer,
          then mix well-dried Ygg leaves, aloe leaves, and royal jelly together with the blue potion,
          add alcohol and let it ferment. If you age it for 12 years, it becomes a 12-year vintage.
          Lightly coat the rim of the glass with yellow spice,
          and serve with royal jelly as a snack.

^800000Heavenly Fruit Juice:^000000 It may feel like you’ll take flight, but you still can’t fly—be careful!
Materials: ^4B00821 Unripe Apple, 1 Orange, 1 Strawberry, 1 Red Spice,
         1 Tropical Banana, 1 Grape Juice, 1 Alcohol^000000
Cooking Method: Peel the apple and orange, then cut them into thirds and quarters respectively.
          Remove the stems from the strawberries, peel the banana, and cut into appropriate sizes.
          Put the cut fruit into a blender, add about half the grape juice, and blend finely.
          When smoothly blended, add the remaining grape juice and alcohol and mix for 30 seconds.
          Pour into a glass and lightly sprinkle red spice.
          If you blend with ice when making the juice, the cold ice helps reduce nutrient loss
          and also improves the taste.

^008040Special Buddha Jumps Over the Wall:^000000 It is absolutely not a stamp made of metal heated in fire and pressed!
Materials: ^4B00821 Meat, 1 Royal Jelly, 1 Sprout, 1 Light Sauce, 1 Yellow Herb,
         1 White Herb, 1 Red Herb^000000
Cooking Method: Soak the meat in refined rice wine for about a day, then steam it for about 2 hours and slice thinly.
          Cut the royal jelly and herbs into easy-to-eat pieces, and blanch the sprouts to prepare.
          Add scallion oil to a pot; when hot, add refined rice wine, broth, seasoning, salt, pepper, and Shaoxing wine,
          then add all prepared ingredients, seal, and steam for about 3 hours to serve.
          

^FF8040Rising Dragon Soup:^000000 With the spicy kick, you feel like a dragon breathing fire from your mouth!
Materials: ^4B00821 Dragon Skin, 1 Dragon Tail, 1 Yggdrasil Leaf, 1 Royal Jelly, 1 Spicy Sauce
          1 Red Spice, 1 Plump Earthworm^000000
Cooking Method: Dragon skin is tough, so score it thoroughly.
          Cut the tail into bite-sized pieces.
          Slice the royal jelly thickly into half-moon shapes, and slice the Yggdrasil leaf diagonally.
          Put the cleanly washed plump earthworm into a pot, add water, and boil vigorously for about 10 minutes.
          When the broth is extracted, remove the earthworm.
          Add spicy sauce and red spice evenly and dissolve into the broth,
          then add the skin, tail, and royal jelly and boil.
          When the broth boils vigorously and the skin and tail are almost cooked,
          add the prepared Ygg leaves, bring to a brief boil once more, and serve.

^400080Blood-Tasting Sparkling Soda:^000000 Said to taste like licking a steel desk.
Materials: ^4B00821 Livestock Blood, 1 Anti Painment, 1 Aloe Vera, 1 Alcohol, 1 Royal Jelly,
        1 Red Spice, 1 Apple Juice^000000
Cooking Method: Finely chop one aloe vera, add warm water at about 20°C, and let it sit for about 5 minutes.
          After 5 minutes, add sugar and the remaining finely chopped aloe vera and water,
          then fill a bottle to about 3.7 liters and shake well for 2 minutes.
          (Shake until all the sugar dissolves.)
          Once dissolved, tighten the lid and let it ferment for about 4–6 days.
          Cut royal jelly into appropriate sizes and blend with apple juice,
          then add the fermented soda water from above along with alcohol and anti painment,
          blend for 30 seconds,
          pour into a glass, and lightly sprinkle red spice.
