What is ^FF0000alcohol^000000?

A general term for compounds in which a hydrogen atom of a hydrocarbon is substituted with a hydroxyl group (-OH).

The general formula is ROH (R = alkyl group). When the alkyl group is methyl, it is called methanol;
when the alkyl group is ethyl, it is called ethanol, and so on, appending -ol to the alkyl group’s name.
A methyl group refers to methane with one hydrogen atom removed,
and an ethyl group refers to ethane with one hydrogen atom removed.
In other words, an alkyl group refers to the original hydrocarbon with one hydrogen atom removed.
