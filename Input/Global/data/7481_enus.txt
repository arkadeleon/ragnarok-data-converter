^FF0000Tongue Dish:^000000 
Materials: ^4B00821 Long, Thin Tongue, 1 Pot, 1 Alcohol, 1 Aloe Vera, 1 Spicy Sauce,
         1 Royal Jelly, 1 Yggdrasil Leaf, 1 Blue Potion^000000
Cooking Method: Put the long, thin tongue and alcohol into a pot, then blanch them in clear broth.
            Slice the aloe vera diagonally and arrange it around the edge of the plate.
            Remove the thin skin, muscles, and tendons from the blanched tongue,
            add an appropriate amount of spicy sauce and toss,
            then plate it on the dish lined with aloe vera.
            If you prefer, you may serve the spicy sauce separately.


^0000FFCocktail: Dragon’s Breath:^000000 A sticky cocktail that somehow feels like it will warm you up!
Materials: ^4B00821 Man-Eating Plant Root, 1 Singing Plant, 1 Aloe Leaf, 1 Royal Jelly, 1 Lemon,
         1 Thorny Fruit, 1 Yggdrasil Fruit, 1 Blue Herb^000000
Cooking Method: Finely grind the well-dried singing plant.
            Slice the man-eating plant root into small pieces and wash it, and wash the thorny fruit clean as well.
            In a kettle or pot, wrap the man-eating plant root with aloe leaves,
            then add it together with the thorny fruit and boil well for at least 2 hours.
            Once sufficiently extracted, remove from heat and strain through a fine sieve or cheesecloth.
            Drink it chilled, or mix it with ice.
            Depending on taste, you may also add royal jelly or lemon.


^800000Hvergelmir’s Liquor:^000000 The legendary “spring-brand” drink! It is absolutely not soy sauce!
Materials: ^4B00821 Yggdrasil Fruit, 1 Ice Piece, 1 Mycelium, 1 Royal Jelly, 1 Concentration Potion,
         1 Alcohol, 1 Thorny Fruit, 1 Illusion Flower^000000
Cooking Method: Wash the Yggdrasil fruit and thorny fruit clean, peel them, and prepare them whole.
            Put mycelium, alcohol, and a well-dried illusion flower into the concentration potion, seal it,
            and ferment for one week.
            After one week, place royal jelly and the peeled fruits into an oak barrel,
            seal it, and age it. (Be careful—flavor changes depending on the aging period.)
            Age for at least 1 year, then strain, bottle, and store.


^008040Desert Scorpion Sand Steam:^000000 It’s scorpion steam, not sand steam!
Materials: ^4B00821 Scorpion Tail, 1 Scorpion Claw, 1 Aloe Vera, 1 Yggdrasil Leaf,
        1 Bitter Herb, 1 Royal Jelly, 1 Spicy Sauce, 1 Pot^000000
Cooking Method: Remove toxins from the scorpion tail, and wash it together with the claw clean.
            Make a marinade by mixing aloe vera juice, finely chopped Yggdrasil leaf, royal jelly, spicy sauce,
            and a small amount of water.
            Soak the tail and claw in the marinade until submerged and marinate for about 10 hours.
            Put it into a pot and seal it tightly, then around 10 a.m.,
            lightly dig into the hot sand of the Morroc Desert under strong sunlight,
            place the pot inside and cover it with sand, then take it out around 5 p.m.
            and plate it.


^FF8040Immortal Stew:^000000 The very dish an ancient eastern emperor sought to live long!
Materials: ^4B00821 Never-Stopping Heart, 1 Dead Man’s Belongings, 1 Yggdrasil Seed, 1 Anti Painment,
         1 Mermaid Heart, 1 Spicy Sauce, 1 Half-Soul, 1 Bitter Herb^000000
Cooking Method: Dice the cleanly washed never-stopping heart carefully, as it may splatter when cut.
            Choose a small mermaid heart, wash it clean, and prepare it.
            First, put the diced never-stopping heart and spicy sauce into a pot,
            stir-fry with finely torn half-soul, and when it is somewhat cooked,
            add anti painment and water, add finely ground dead man’s belongings, and boil.
            When it starts to boil, add the mermaid heart and simmer a little more until it cooks through.
            When the mermaid heart is somewhat cooked, add shredded bitter herb and Yggdrasil seed
            and bring to a brief boil once more.


^400080Nine-Tailed Fox Tail Dish:^000000 For the day you finally cook a Moonlight Flower tail!
Materials: ^4B00821 Fox Tail, 1 Four-Leaf Clover, 1 Man-Eating Plant Root, 1 Sharp Leaf,
         1 Yggdrasil Leaf, 1 Light Sauce, 1 Yellow Spice, 1 Isidor^000000
Cooking Method: Peel the fox tail, rinse it lightly in water, then blanch it in boiling water
            to remove fat.
            Wash the man-eating plant root clean and trim it into small pieces,
            and blanch the sharp leaf lightly and cut it in half.
            Put the blanched tail into a marinade made with finely chopped Yggdrasil leaf, light sauce, Isidor,
            and yellow spice, toss evenly, and let it absorb flavor well.
            Place the seasoned tail together with the remaining marinade in a pot,
            add 1 cup of broth, and boil.
            When it has boiled to a certain point, reduce the heat and let it steam gently,
            add the four-leaf clover, and when the marinade has almost reduced away, remove from heat.
            (Be careful—if you add the four-leaf clover too early, it turns yellow and looks unappealing.)
