----------------Foreword--------------------

Since alcohol production became known to the world, it has been used for various purposes.
While searching for a more efficient and convenient method for military use,
we discovered a way to maximize its effectiveness—the Molotov cocktail.
As it was created for military purposes, its use by civilians is dangerous.
I leave this record in the hope that this document does not spread outside and cause unfortunate incidents.

------------------------------------------



What is a ^0000FFMolotov Cocktail^000000?

A type of simple incendiary grenade made by filling a glass bottle with an incendiary agent.



Materials: ^4B00821 Alcohol, 1 Transparent Cloth Piece, 1 Empty Bottle^000000
Manufacturing Method: Prepare a mortar.
                    Carefully pour alcohol into the mortar and allow it to slightly oxidize through contact with air.
                    Pour the alcohol back into the empty bottle, soak one end of the transparent cloth in the alcohol,
                    tie the other end once, and use the tied part to seal the mouth of the bottle.
                    For easier manufacturing, carry this book, prepare the materials,
                    and use Pharmacy, the basic skill of alchemy.
