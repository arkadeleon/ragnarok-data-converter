What is a ^FF0000Slim Potion^000000?


A highly concentrated potion that delivers the same effectiveness as existing potions with only a small amount.
Naturally, it is also much more convenient to carry.



^0000FFRed Slim Potion^000000: Restores approximately 45 HP.
Materials: ^4B00821 Red Potion, 1 Sharp Cactus Needle, 1 Empty Test Tube^000000
Manufacturing Method: Prepare a mortar.
          Slowly pour in the red potion.
          Grind the well-dried sharp cactus needle and mix it with the red potion,
          then pour it into an empty test tube.
          For a simpler method,
          prepare this book and the materials and use the Pharmacy skill.


^800000Yellow Slim Potion^000000: Restores approximately 175 HP.
Materials: ^4B00821 Yellow Potion, 1 Mole Whisker, 1 Empty Test Tube^000000
Manufacturing Method: Prepare a mortar.
          Slowly pour in the yellow potion.
          Add water in which mole whiskers have been thoroughly boiled and mix it with the potion.
          Pour into an empty test tube.
          For a simpler method,
          prepare this book and the materials and use the Pharmacy skill.


^008040White Slim Potion^000000: Restores approximately 325 HP.
Materials: ^4B00821 White Potion, 1 Witch’s Star Sand, 1 Empty Test Tube^000000
Manufacturing Method: Prepare a mortar.
          Slowly pour in the white potion.
          Thoroughly wash the witch’s star sand, remove all moisture,
          then gradually add it while mixing.
          Pour into an empty test tube.
          For a simpler method,
          prepare this book and the materials and use the Pharmacy skill.
