----------------Foreword--------------------
The monster known as the Marine Sphere, discovered while exploring Izlude,
did not move, but possessed the characteristic of exploding when touched.
We wondered if there was a way to suspend it in the air rather than underwater,
and eventually, we succeeded in synthesizing it!
We believe this bomb, when used against enemies, will be advantageous for ambushes.
------------------------------------------

What is a ^400080Mine^000000?

A type of bomb that floats in midair or underwater and sends targets flying upon contact.
A mine bottle is created by processing this bomb in a special way and storing it in a bottle.
Extreme caution is required from manufacturing to usage.
However, only alchemists who have mastered specialized techniques
are said to be able to use it.


Materials: ^4B00821 Detonator, 1 Tendon, 1 Empty Bottle^000000
Manufacturing Method: Prepare a mortar.
          Place the detonator and tendon into the mortar, and while carrying this book,
          use Pharmacy to bottle the result into an empty bottle.
          Unlike the synthesis used to create mines,
          a separate skill must be learned in order to use it.
