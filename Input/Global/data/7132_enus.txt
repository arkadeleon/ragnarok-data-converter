What is ^400080Coating^000000?

The act of covering the surface of an object with a thin film of resin or similar material.
It is used to create anti-reflective coatings on lenses,
or to apply waterproof or heat-resistant processing to fabrics.
However, coating used in alchemy means making something absolutely unbreakable,
and ensuring that opponents cannot forcibly remove it.
Nevertheless, only alchemists who have mastered specialized techniques
are said to be able to use it.


Materials: ^4B00821 Alcohol, 1 Zenorc Fang, 1 Mermaid Heart, 1 Empty Bottle^000000
Manufacturing Method: Prepare a mortar.
          Pour about half the alcohol into the mortar and dissolve the Zenorc fang.
          Clean, dry, and grind the mermaid heart, then mix it thoroughly into the alcohol.
          Use Pharmacy to bottle the mixture into an empty bottle.
          Although it may seem like simply applying it is enough,
          in reality, a special skill must be used to coat weapons or armor.
