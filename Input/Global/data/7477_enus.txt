^FF0000Shining Neobiani:^000000 Don’t worry—it doesn’t shine so brightly that you can’t eat it!
Materials: ^4B00821 Meat, 1 Honey, 1 Yellow Herb, 1 Mastela Fruit, 1 Shining Scale^000000
Cooking Method: Remove bones from the meat and tenderize it thoroughly with a meat mallet.
          Place the meat on a wide plate and generously drizzle honey and Mastela fruit juice.
          Pat it by hand so the seasoning soaks in well, then season lightly with salt and pepper.
          Wrap the seasoned meat well with the shining scale and marinate for about a day.
          Heat a grill rack or frying pan until hot, spread the marinated meat out, and grill.
          Line a slightly concave wide plate with yellow herb and plate the grilled meat.

^0000FFRed Mushroom Wine:^000000 Rest assured—it’s not that mushrooms grew because it was stored in a damp place!
Materials: ^4B00821 Edible Mushroom, 1 Grape Juice, 1 Mushroom Spore, 1 Alcohol, 1 Red Spice^000000
Cooking Method: Finely chop the edible mushroom.
          Pour an appropriate amount of grape juice into a container,
          sprinkle sliced mushrooms and spores little by little, layering them,
          and let it sit for about 1 to 3 days.
          Add a small amount of alcohol and red spice, seal the lid tightly,
          then steep and age in a cool, dark place for about 3 months.
          When aging is complete, remove the solids, filter until clear,
          then bottle it to finish.

^800000Peach Cake:^000000 The key is bringing out a soft peach flavor!
Materials: ^4B00821 Very Hard Peach, 1 Slice of Cake, 1 Cheese, 1 Milk,
         1 Orange Juice, 1 Sweet Sauce^000000
Cooking Method: Prepare by leaving an appropriate amount of the slice cake’s sponge portion,
          then gulp down the rest.
          Slice the peach thinly into half-moon shapes.
          Put sweet sauce and a little cheese into a pot and melt,
          add the sliced peaches, simmer until softened, then cool at room temperature.
          Butter a cake mold, sprinkle sugar, and set aside.
          Finely chop the remaining cheese and the slice cake and mix,
          then add eggs and milk and mix a bit more.
          Pour the cooled peach compote together with syrup into the mold,
          then pour the batter on top.
          (Decoration is up to your preference.)
          Bake at 160°C for 50 minutes to enjoy the taste of a soft peach cake.



^008040Chili Shrimp Gratin:^000000
Materials: ^4B00821 Red Chili, 1 Shrimp, 1 Sweet Sauce, 1 Lemon^000000
Cooking Method: Peel the shrimp and prepare; juice the lemon using a squeezer.
          Grind chili powder finely.
          In a pan, add 5 tablespoons of water to the finely ground chili powder and add the shrimp to stir-fry,
          then stir-fry a bit more while adding sweet sauce and lemon juice.
          Butter a gratin dish, add the stir-fried shrimp, top with cheese,
          bake for about 10 minutes in an oven preheated to 180°C,
          then top with sliced lemon and serve.
              

^FF8040Extremely Bitter Tonic:^000000 Medicine to the pharmacist, treatment to the doctor...
Materials: ^4B00821 Brown Root, 1 Man-Eating Plant Root, 1 Mushroom Spore, 1 Edible Mushroom, 1 Honey^000000
Cooking Method: Dry the herbs well and slice them thinly with scissors or similar.
          Put them into a decoction pot (or glass kettle), pour in 4–5 cups of cold water,
          and wait 1–2 hours for the herbs to swell.
          Then simmer gently over the lowest heat, and when the water begins to bubble against the herbs, turn off the heat.
          Strain through fine cloth without squeezing, and drink only the clear decoction.
          For a second brew, add water to the strained herbs and boil again.


^400080Geoun-tang:^000000 A huge stroke of luck made purely by luck. It’s a (hot) lucky soup!
Materials: ^4B00821 Reptile Tongue, 1 Long Thin Tongue, 1 Clover, 1 Witch’s Star Sand, 1 Aloe^000000
Cooking Method: Blanch the reptile tongue and long thin tongue, rinse in cold water, and shred.
          Peel the aloe and shred it.
          Put the witch’s star sand into a wide pot and boil,
          add the shredded tongues, and when the broth boils vigorously and they are somewhat cooked,
          add clover and boil a little longer.
